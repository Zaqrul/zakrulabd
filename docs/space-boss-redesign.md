# Chapter boss Canvas redesign

All eight chapter bosses now use flat Canvas shapes, dark outlines, muted military colours, articulated machinery and readable weapon openings. These are adaptations to the existing Space game, not imported sprites or exact replicas.

| Chapter | New design | Visual reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Siege Tank: stacked armour, tracks, large cannon | Iron Nokana / Tani-Oh |
| 2 | Iron Flagship: low amphibious hull, bridge, three turrets | Big Shiee |
| 3 | Artillery Hermit: armoured shell, jointed pincers | Huge Hermit |
| 4 | Hive Carrier: alien saucer, weapon arms, hanging machinery | Rugname and Metal Slug alien craft |
| 5 | Iron Leviathan: linked metal segments and armoured head | Iron Death Worm |
| 6 | Solar Bomber: folding wings and engine pods | Flying Tara / Metal Slug military aircraft |
| 7 | Crimson Fang: Vex's own ace fighter, forward-swept blade wings, canopy with the pilot, tracking chin cannon (replaces the Iron Knight robot) | Original design |
| 8 | Dreadnought: cockpit, rotor, missile doors, exposed engine | Hi-Do |

The detailed siege tank is the visual standard for all chapters: bevelled armour planes, inset panels, hydraulic joints and segmented weapon housings use flat colours. Bosses are enlarged by 30–65% depending on the chapter, while the player's ship is 15% smaller. Physical body dimensions, weapon mounts, worm segments and the Crimson Fang's charge lance follow the new scale. Boss drift is limited to preserve screen clearance. Damage changes the appearance by phase; reduced motion freezes decorative movement. Boss names and relevant dialogue are updated in both languages.
