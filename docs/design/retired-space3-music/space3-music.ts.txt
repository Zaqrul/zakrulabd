/* Space 3's music: run-and-gun fusion meets an orchestral-rock traveller's score, and the two games before it. The drive
   is a late-90s arcade shooter (brass hooks, a jazz interlude inside a metal tune, a guitar solo, a key that lifts); the
   heart is a JRPG battle theme (a drumless prelude, a lyrical middle, a chorus in a new key); the chip of Space 1 and the
   brass and strings of Space 2 turn up inside it (space3-motifs.ts). One theme runs through the whole album, the Gate
   theme, the hook of Phobos Run: a rising pickup to a held fifth, and a falling answer. Every tune treats it its own way
   (a march, a lullaby, a swung head, a coiled riff, a glacier) the way Metal Slug brings its main theme back later in the
   series in a bigger arrangement. Every tune is written for this game; none copies a note from anything else.
   Notation is Space 2's (see space2-music.ts); the reasoning is in docs/space3-music-research.md. */
